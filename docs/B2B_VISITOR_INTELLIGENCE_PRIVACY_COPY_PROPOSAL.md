# B2B Visitor Intelligence Privacy Copy Proposal

This proposal is intentionally not wired into the Privacy page in this
parallel worktree.

Suggested copy for legal review:

> We may process limited website access information, such as page paths,
> referrer and campaign information, to understand business use of the site
> and improve follow-up. Where enabled, a server-side service may classify a
> network at company level using a hashed network identifier and company
> enrichment data. This does not identify a particular person or confirm who
> visited the site. The information may be inaccurate and automated records
> are treated as candidates for human review, not as customer inquiries.

> We do not ask the visitor-event endpoint to collect names, email addresses,
> phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers, inquiry messages, attachments, passwords,
> payment data, or a client-submitted IP address. A real inquiry submitted
> through the Contact form is handled separately and may contain the details
> the visitor chooses to provide.

> You may object to or ask about this processing using the published contact
> route. We will review the request under the law applicable to the relevant
> processing and deployment.

Required follow-up: legal review before any real provider is enabled in
Production. `PRIVACY_PAGE_RUNTIME_CHANGE=DEFERRED_TO_INTEGRATION`.
